# Smart Retail AI Platform: Dataset Methodology

**Version:** 1.2.0  
**Status:** Verified & Fully Reproducible  
**Generator Script:** `scripts/generate_datasets.py`  
**Fixed Random Seed:** `42`

---

## 1. Motivation & Guiding Principles

To provide an ethical, self-contained, and 100% reproducible software release:
1. **Privacy & Licensing Cleanliness:** No scraped proprietary images, unverified public faces, or copyrighted datasets are embedded.
2. **Deterministic Reproducibility:** Every single data point, label, and split is synthesized using pseudo-random number generators seeded with `seed=42`.
3. **Structured Stratification:** Datasets are rigorously divided into isolated training (`data/training/`) and evaluation (`data/evaluation/`) directories to prevent data leakage.
4. **Challenge Sets:** Each module includes an adversarial/challenge evaluation partition designed to probe model boundaries.

---

## 2. Product Image Classification Dataset

### 2.1 Synthesis Technique
Synthetic images are rendered at $128 \times 128 \times 3$ resolution using OpenCV drawing primitives and uniform sensor noise:
- **`bottle`:** Vertical elongated rectangular body, neck narrowing, cap structure, and subtle blue/cyan bottle tinting.
- **`mug`:** Cylindrical container base with an outer elliptical handle on the right perimeter.
- **`headphones`:** Symmetrical headband arc with dual left and right padded circular earcup features.
- **`shoe`:** Horizontal sole line with a multi-segment polygonal upper mimicking a sneaker profile.

### 2.2 Dataset Partitions
- **Training Partition (`data/training/product/`):**
  - 60 images per category across 4 categories = **240 samples**.
- **Evaluation Partition (`data/evaluation/product/`):**
  - 15 images per category across 4 categories = **60 samples** (80/20 train/test split).
- **Challenge Partition (`data/evaluation/product_challenge/`):**
  - 5 images per category = **20 samples**. Rendered with $2\times$ pixel noise amplitude ($\pm 30$ intensity levels) and random spatial translation offsets ($\pm 3$ pixels).
- **Manifest:** All image paths and labels are indexed in `data/product_manifest.csv`.

## 3. Customer Sentiment Analysis Dataset

### 3.1 Lexical Generation Process
Customer feedback is generated by combining parameterized syntactic frames with real-world retail vocabulary across delivery, product durability, packaging, and support domains.

Natural variation injection appends conversational sentence closers (e.g. *"Overall good"*, *"Needs attention"*, *"Very impressed"*) at a 40% probability rate.

### 3.2 Partition Breakdown
- **Training Set (`data/training/sentiment_train.csv`):**
  - Negative: 94 samples
  - Neutral: 95 samples
  - Positive: 94 samples
  - **Total:** 283 balanced samples.
- **Test Set (`data/evaluation/sentiment_test.csv`):**
  - Negative: 31 samples
  - Neutral: 32 samples
  - Positive: 32 samples
  - **Total:** 95 held-out samples.
- **Challenge Set (`data/evaluation/sentiment_challenge.csv`):**
  - 20 handcrafted complex reviews featuring idioms (*"Zero stars if I could"*), double negatives (*"Not bad at all"*), mixed polarities (*"Super fast shipping, but the product itself is completely broken"*), and sarcasm.

## 4. Face Verification Evaluation Set

### 4.1 Gallery & Probe Generation
- **Registered Gallery Identity (`data/training/face/person_01_reference.png`):**
  - Canonical synthetic facial portrait containing detectable geometric facial landmarks (forehead, eye sockets, nose bridge, mouth contour) on an equalized grayscale canvas.
- **Positive Probe (`data/evaluation/face/positive_query.png`):**
  - Same individual captured under a 5% positive illumination shift to simulate real-world ambient sensor variations.
- **Negative Probe (`data/evaluation/face/negative_other_face.png`):**
  - Distinct individual with different facial dimensions and feature geometries to verify false-acceptance rejection.
- **Distractor Probe (`data/evaluation/face/no_face_distractor.png`):**
  - Geometric non-face canvas to verify that the detector cleanly reports `No face detected` rather than hallucinating an identity.
